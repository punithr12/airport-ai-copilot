# LAX Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-PRICE-001

## Airport
LAX

## Purpose
Define pricing and surge rules for airport ride activity at LAX.

## Standard Pricing

LAX rides should operate under the standard marketplace pricing model when supply and demand are within normal operating conditions.

## Surge Multiplier

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at LAX is 1.8x.

The current surge multiplier must be considered before proposing any surge adjustment.

Surge adjustments must not exceed the maximum permitted multiplier of 1.8x.

## Surge Increase Conditions

A surge increase may be considered when operational conditions indicate insufficient supply relative to demand.

Relevant indicators include:

- Low completion rate
- High driver cancellation rate
- Large airport queue
- Increased average ETA
- High request volume
- Insufficient active drivers

## Surge Approval

Increasing surge may require approval depending on the proposed multiplier and applicable project risk classification.

Any proposed surge increase must first be validated against the maximum permitted surge of 1.8x.

A proposed surge above 1.8x is not permitted under this policy.
