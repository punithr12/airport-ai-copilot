# SFO Driver Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-DRIVER-001

## Airport
SFO

## Purpose
Define driver operating and incentive guidelines for SFO airport trips.

## Airport Queue Rules

Drivers entering the SFO airport staging area must join the designated driver queue.

Drivers should remain in the queue while waiting for airport trip requests.

Drivers must not intentionally abandon the queue to bypass the normal request allocation process.

## Driver Eligibility

Drivers must comply with applicable airport operating requirements to remain eligible for airport trip requests.

Repeated queue abandonment or violations of airport operating procedures may affect eligibility for subsequent airport requests.

## Driver Cancellation

Drivers should avoid cancelling accepted airport trips unless cancellation is necessary.

A high driver cancellation rate should be treated as an operational signal during airport marketplace investigations.

## Driver Incentives

Additional driver incentives may be considered when available driver supply is insufficient to meet airport demand.

Potential indicators for considering an incentive include:

- Low completion rate
- High request volume
- Large airport queue
- High average ETA
- Low active-driver availability

## Incentive Recommendation

The incentive amount should be determined using:

- Number of drivers required
- Operational severity
- Current supply-demand conditions
- Applicable approval requirements

## Operational Monitoring

When an incentive is recommended, the operations team should monitor:

- Active drivers
- Completion rate
- Driver cancellation rate
- Request volume
- Average ETA
- Queue size
