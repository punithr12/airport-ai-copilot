# JFK Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-OPS-001

## Airport
JFK

## Purpose
Define operational guidelines for airport ride activity at JFK.

## Completion Rate

The expected minimum trip completion rate at JFK is 83%.

If the completion rate falls below 83%, the airport marketplace should be investigated for operational disruption.

A completion rate below 73% is classified as a critical operational condition.

## Driver Queue

Drivers must remain in the designated airport queue after entering the approved staging area.

Drivers should not abandon the queue while waiting for an eligible airport request.

Queue abandonment may reduce driver eligibility for subsequent airport requests.

## Pickup Operations

Drivers may pick up riders only from designated airport pickup zones.

Drivers must follow airport pickup instructions and should not perform pickups from unauthorized locations.

## Drop-off Operations

Drivers may drop off riders at approved passenger drop-off areas.

Drivers should follow airport traffic and staging instructions during drop-off operations.

## Operational Investigation

When JFK operational performance deteriorates, the following metrics should be investigated:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Active drivers
- Request volume
- Current surge multiplier

## Escalation

Operational conditions requiring intervention should be reviewed by the appropriate operations team before sensitive marketplace actions are performed.
